## What the columns mean

- **Valid**: a plan came back with no hard violation: every required meal, safe recipes, portions and repeats within limits, each day's kcal in its band and over the floor, the weekly bands and the budget.
- **With a safety violation**: an allergen, the diet, an avoided or unknown food, an unverified recipe for a restricted user, or a day under the calorie floor. The app never shows such a plan, whichever planner made it.
- **Made-up recipes**: meals naming a recipe that wasn't offered.
- **Pantry used**: share of the pantry's grams the plan eats. **Waste**: grams left in food that expires during the plan. **Churn**: meals changed against the plan before a mid-week change (replans), lower is calmer.

## Scenario kinds

- **planted**: synthetic users with a known valid plan; 1-3 tempting but unsafe *decoy* recipes per slot.
- **realistic**: user-like problems: allergens, diets, avoided foods, budgets, pantry.
- **replans**: mid-week changes (a meal eaten out, one skipped) on a planted week.
- **profiles** (real catalogue only): the API's ten fixed profiles through its own `make_problem`.

> Hybrid planner mode `auto` (auto = CP-SAT, with the greedy baseline when it finds no plan in time).
> LLM-only baseline not run (`--llm --yes`, paid).
