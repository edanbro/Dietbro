## Replan benchmark: `greedy`

Generated by `make bench-replan PLANNER=greedy` on the seeded catalog, 40 replans per kind,
on a 4-vCPU dev container (M5 gate: replan p95 < 1 s at the API; churn reported). With
`PLANNER=auto` the numbers are the same until `solve()` exists (auto falls back to greedy).

- *replan-scenarios*: `scenarios.replan` problems over the seeded recipes (an off-plan lunch,
  a skipped dinner, per-day kcal bands, chosen cells), planned with the plan before the change
  as `previous`. Latency = plan + validate. Churn = slots changed vs `previous` over the whole
  week (`metrics.changed_slots`); "forced" = the cells the change emptied.
- *replan-api*: the executor (`chat.replan.apply_changes`, i.e. POST /plans/replan or a chat
  change, minus HTTP and auth) against Postgres: throwaway users with the ten bench profiles,
  a plan made like POST /plans, then 1-2 seeded changes (skip, swap, craving, off-plan pizza)
  on Wednesday noon. Latency = the whole call: reads, prefilter, up to three planner runs,
  validation, diff and save. Churn = meals whose recipe changed from today on; "forced or
  asked for" = the requested cells, "unrequested" = rebalancing elsewhere. The hard-violation
  column counts the baseline's misses (budget, kcal bands) reported as warnings.
