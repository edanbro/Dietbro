# What people actually eat

Why the planner plans what it plans (ADR-0019). Read on 2026-10-30. Most consumer surveys here
are paid for by food brands and differ in method and year, so they are rough rankings, not
precise shares. The Irish NANS study and the national snacking surveys are the firmer evidence.

## The shape of a day

- **Ireland, 1,500 adults, four-day food diaries (NANS, 2008-10).** The most common weekday
  pattern, 60% of records, is cereal or toast for breakfast (0.88), a sandwich or nothing for
  the light meal, and a main meal of protein and carbohydrate (0.72). A cooked breakfast is
  about a tenth of weekdays and a fifth of weekends.
  [Generic meal patterns, Nutrients 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5872728/)
- **So:** a main meal has protein and carbohydrate; a lunch is lighter; soups, salads and
  rice, potato or pasta dishes fill in around the sandwich.

## Breakfast

- Cereal 23%, toast 18%, porridge 15% (2,000 UK adults).
  [Weetabix](https://weetabix.co.uk/great-british-breakfast/)
- Cereal 36.5%, toast 28.4%, porridge 12.5% (1,686 adults, 2013).
  [Robertson's survey](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/the-death-of-deskfast-197932691.html)
- Cereal, then toast, a cooked breakfast, porridge.
  [AA](https://thecaterer.com/news/aa-reveals-cereal-as-the-nations-favourite-breakfast)

## Lunch

- Sandwich or wrap 19%, fish and chips 9%, soup 8%, jacket potato 7% (Foodhub, via
  [Bakery Info](https://bakeryinfo.co.uk/news/wraps-and-flatbreads-oust-sandwiches-at-lunch/625536.article)).
- Salad 42%, wrap 37%, soup 24%, pasta 24%, sandwich 15%, leftovers 11% (1,133 mostly London
  workers, "ever eat" style question).
  [Spitalfields, via City AM](https://www.cityam.com/?p=15161)
- The usual repeat lunch is a ham sandwich, then cheese, then chicken.
  [Coventry Telegraph](https://www.coventrytelegraph.net/news/business/you-eat-same-lunch-every-12643245)

## Dinner

- Ireland, Bord Bia food diaries (2019), adult evening meals, in order: meat and two veg (11%),
  a chicken dish (10%), pasta with sauce (10%), pizza or lasagne, burger or steak and chips, a
  fish dish, a light meal, Chinese, stew or casserole, Indian. Potatoes in 28% of meals, red
  meat in 29%, fish 7%.
  [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/life-and-style/food-and-drink/red-meat-still-dominates-irish-dinners-research-shows-1.4162531)
- UK, Tesco: roast dinner, spaghetti bolognese, chicken with vegetables, pizza, stir fry,
  toasties, fish and chips, shepherd's pie, lasagne, sausage and mash.
  [The Star](https://www.thestar.co.uk/lifestyle/food-and-drink/can-you-guess-what-tops-the-list-of-the-nations-favourite-home-cooked-meal-4273108)

## Snacks

- Snacks are about a fifth of the day's energy across national surveys: 19.5-22.4% in the US
  (NHANES 2005-16), 21% in Brazil at 1.6 snacks a day, 20% in Australia.
  [NHANES, via Fox](https://www.foxnews.com/health/snacks-pack-more-calories-than-meal-us-adults-study-finds),
  [Brazil](https://www.nature.com/articles/ejcn201360),
  [Australia](https://researchportal.scu.edu.au/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Contribution-of-dietary-snacking-behaviours-to/991013120012402368)
- 91% of adults in 13 countries snack every day, and two thirds have biscuits at least weekly.
  [Mondelez, via Food Industry Executive](https://foodindustryexecutive.com/2019/11/snacks-are-officially-the-new-meals-mondelez-report-finds/)
- What dietitians suggest: whole fruit, nuts, eggs, cottage cheese, popcorn, with a mix of
  protein, fat and carbohydrate.
  [To Your Health](https://chirofind.com/mpacms/tyh/article.php?id=3303)
